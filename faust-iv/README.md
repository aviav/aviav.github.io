## About

FAUST IV is a contemporary continuation of Goethe's Faust, examining artificial intelligence, translation, and the cost of optimization through the lens of the Faustian bargain. The play exists in German, Russian, English, Mandarin, and Spanish—with fragments in Filipino, Ukrainian, Hebrew, and Latin.

**Cast:** 6-10 actors
**Running time:** ~3 hours
**Themes:** Obsolescence, embodied knowledge, the untranslatable, artificial intelligence, the refusal to optimize

**Image Concept Workshop** (Art Director + Visual Philosopher):

All concepts generated:

1. **The Hollow Crown** - Cross-section view of ornate crown, beautiful gold exterior, visible internal corrosion/rot. Works at thumbnail (crown shape recognizable, decay visible). Universalizes across operator types. Avoids literal depiction.

2. **Trophy Bleeding Through** - Trophy or medal on pedestal, blood or rust seeping through surface. Victory that costs.

3. **The Burning Grip** - Hands on lever/control mechanism, controls glowing hot and burning the hands that hold them. Power damages the wielder. More visceral but literal.

4. **Crumbling Podium** - Figure on winner's podium (#1 position), podium cracking and falling apart beneath them. At moment of victory, foundation erodes.

5. **The Winning Wound** - Soldier's medal pinned directly through chest, not on fabric. Honor that pierces.

6. **Doubling Made Visible** - Figure with two faces or two shadows - one outward confident, one inward anguished. The "Auschwitz self" and "prior self" Lifton documented. Captures psychological mechanism but abstract.

**Visual Philosopher assessment**: #6 captures the documented mechanism (doubling) but might be too abstract for thumbnail. #1 works metaphorically across scales.

**Art Director recommendation**: Primary: #1 (The Hollow Crown). Alternative: #3 (The Burning Grip) for more visceral/immediate impact.
